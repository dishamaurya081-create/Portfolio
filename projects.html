Nagar AI

Core Problem: Citizen grievances in dense metropolitan areas like Mumbai suffer from slow categorization, lack of objective visual proof, and fragmented municipal dispatch.

An end-to-end AI-powered Mumbai civic intelligence platform designed to route citizen grievances, generate real-time waterlogging heatmaps, and automate before-and-after visual proof verification of civic maintenance.

Key System Capabilities:
  • Ward Pulse Map: Real-time spatial tracking of ward complaints and localized crisis zones.
  • Gemini Vision Verification: Multi-modal AI comparing repair imagery against prior condition before closing tickets.
  • Civic Risk Index & Rewards: Automated priority dispatch algorithms coupled with citizen incentive tokens.
  • Audited PDF Export: Instant generation of municipal audit reports with formatted metadata via jsPDF.

ClassMate

Core Problem: College engineering timetables are notoriously convoluted with alternating lab batches, electives, and sudden room switches that generic calendar apps fail to track accurately.

A tailored timetable and academic scheduling engine designed specifically for ABES Engineering College students, featuring section/group timetable routing, real-time class countdowns, offline cache, and web push notifications.

Key System Capabilities:
  • Section/Track Hierarchy: Granular department, semester, section, and lab group timetable mapping.
  • Real-time State Machine: Dynamic calculation of current lecture, upcoming lab, break countdown, and room numbers.
  • Offline-First PWA: Service worker caching guaranteeing instant schedule display even in basement lecture halls with no cellular reception.
  • Web Push & Theme Toggle: Proactive 5-minute pre-class warnings and adaptive high-contrast dark theme.

Privacy Guardian AI

Core Problem: End users routinely accept hundreds of pages of legal Terms of Service without knowing their data retention, cross-platform tracking, or forced arbitration terms.

An intelligent Terms of Service analyzer that dissects legal jargon and translates policies into an interactive privacy health score, risk clause highlights, side-by-side policy comparisons, and conversational Q&A.

Key System Capabilities:
  • Privacy Health Score: Calibrated algorithmic assessment out of 100 with weighted risk penalties.
  • Risk-Clause Breakdown: Pinpoints clauses for data brokers, biometric tracking, and arbitration.
  • Compare Mode: Side-by-side multi-policy delta analysis for competitor app evaluation.
  • Grounded AI Assistant & PDF Export: Chat interface answering specific questions directly from verified document text.
